Essential Shamanism

Essential Shamanism is the living practice of the ancient axiom "know yourself." It is a non-dogmatic path that unites mystical non-dual traditions. The focus is on revealing our true nature, expanding consciousness, and cultivating mindfulness, unconditional love, compassion, equanimity, and a deep joy of living. These teachings are designed to be applied in everyday life, offering practical tools for inner transformation.

Conscious Breathing

In this retreat, we will explore breathing as a transformative path. Through Anapanasati Yoga, a Buddhist meditation practice that uses breath to calm the mind and sharpen awareness, and Pranayama, an ancient technique that balances vital energy, participants will foster both physical and emotional healing. We will also engage in the Sufi Hadra, a collective ritual that combines rhythmic breathing with sacred litanies to open the doors to altered states of consciousness and mystical communion.

Voice Work

The voice will be a central focus of this retreat. Through sacred singing, participants will learn to use the power of vibration as a tool for healing and spiritual connection. The retreat will include initiations into ancestral chants, passed down for centuries and used in sacred rituals to deepen spiritual experience and connection.

Initiation into Rituals

As part of the retreat, participants will also be initiated into shamanic rituals for energetic purification, offering, and gratitude. Traditional practices like Sampai Zen, the beautiful manifestation of the spirit of gratitude, will help us recognize all beings, honour the Earth that sustains us, and accept reality as it is.

Ayahuasca:

A Deeper Preparation

This set of teachings not only offers invaluable tools to enhance daily life but also serves as an ideal preparation for a deeper work with Mother Ayahuasca. By engaging in these practices, participants arrive at the ceremony in a state of balance and openness, enhancing the healing power of the medicine. These teachings prepare you for the profound journey of Ayahuasca, allowing for a deeper connection and heightened transformative effects during the ceremony.
